The method

How to play

A turn-based stealth game that keeps the three tables from the original rulebook.

I · Get in

Over the wall

Each thief has 6 action points: one point per step, or per column of the break-in table. Jump the wall and risk spraining an ankle, or force the gate.

II · Break in

Open it quietly

Doors, windows, wardrobes and the safe can all be forced. The faster you go, the more noise you make: the sound ripple is shown before every move. If a guard or a sleeper hears it, the alarm goes off.

III · Grab

Roll the dice

The dice roll on screen for the loot, the jumps and the fights. A ring, a necklace, a bundle of compromising letters… and maybe Madame’s diamonds.

The thieves

The gang

Three thieves, three ways in. Hover over or tap a portrait to see their card.

Facing them

The guards

  • Their rounds go straight ahead. At every obstacle, a die picks their new direction.
  • They cover 6 spaces per turn, and 12 from the first alert until the end.
  • You only see the rooms your thieves are in. A guard out of sight can be heard from 6 spaces away.
  • Sleepers are hidden among the furniture. If your noise reaches them, they raise the alarm, just like a guard.
