Twelve sharp. Things get messy.

The shepherd’s pie is cold, the rillettes are flying low and the supervisor has his whistle out. Your gang of six must be the last one standing when the bell rings.

6pupils in your gang

How to play

A turn-based tactics game, like a board game come to life.

1 · Move

Place your pupils

Each pupil has 12 action points: a step costs 1 point, a throw costs 4. Climb onto a table to get a better aim.

2 · Aim

Roll the dice

Two dice pick the dish. Your dexterity has to beat the distance plus the dish’s difficulty. A hit, and the target loses morale.

3 · Hold on

Stock up

After four turns the plates are empty: dash to the kitchen, its food trays and its trolleys… if the cook lets you through.

The gang

Six pupils, six personalities. Hover over or tap a pupil to see their character sheet.

And facing them…

The supervisor

The grown-ups aren’t laughing

The supervisor comes in through a random door and marches off the nearest pupil. The cook defends his kitchen and dunks intruders in the dish of the day. And neutral pupils who get hit may join your gang… or go completely berserk.

Installation help

Browser: a recent Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari, preferably on a computer. Windows: unzip, then run delire_a_la_cantine.exe; if Windows says it “protected your PC”, click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Linux: chmod +x delire_a_la_cantine.x86_64, then run it.

Based on the board game Délire à la cantine by Didier Guiserix and François Marcela-Froideval, which came with issue no. 14 of the French magazine Jeux & Stratégie in 1982.
