The insert games, replayed as video games

For ten years, a French magazine slipped a complete board game between its pages. We are bringing some of them back, to be played on screen.

The magazine

Jeux & Stratégie was sold on French newsstands from 1980 to 1990. A magazine for everyone, curious about anything that can be played: board and card games, chess and go, wargames, role-playing games, the first computer games, not to mention the riddles, puzzles and mazes that filled so many evenings.

Its trademark was the insert (l’encart). Issue after issue, an original game came with the magazine: a board to unfold, counters to cut out and a few pages of rules. People played it with their family, at school or with friends, and for many it was their very first strategy game.

2026

The magazine is back

Thirty-six years after the last issue, a team led by Émilien Guillon launched a crowdfunding campaign on Ulule on 2 October 2026 to bring Jeux & Stratégie back to life: a 116-page print issue, true to the original spirit, from riddles to wargames.
